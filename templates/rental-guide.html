Rental Terms & Criteria

We Value Our Tenants!

  1. 01

    Rental Criteria

    Additional terms and criteria for most of our rentals:

    Gross monthly income must be three times greater than the rental amount.

    May need to provide several years of verifiable residences.

    Need 2 verifiable landlords. No evictions or landlord collections.

    No felonies (no exceptions).

    If your rental criteria is weak, you must have a reliable co-signer. The income requirement for a co-signer is six times the rent.

    Reside Here runs a local and national criminal background check, eviction search, and a credit check for all residents over the age of 18.

  2. 02

    Rental Terms

    Security Deposit Equal to Rent.

    Security Deposit & First Month Rent Due at Move-in.

    $200 of deposit is non-refundable.

    12 Month Lease.

  3. 03

    Rental Application
