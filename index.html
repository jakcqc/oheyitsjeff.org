A few things you might be wondering.

How do I apply for a home?+

Start with our current rental listings. Choose a home and select “Apply Now” on its listing. Review the rental criteria before applying, or call our team if you’d like help.

Can I schedule a showing?+

Of course. Call (618) 235-8400 during office hours, Monday–Friday, 9 a.m.–5:30 p.m., to talk with our team about a showing.

Are pets welcome?+

Pet policies vary by property. Check the individual rental listing for current details, or contact our team before applying.

How do I request maintenance?+

For routine requests, sign in to the resident portal. We also provide 24-hour emergency maintenance; use the emergency contact instructions provided with your lease or resident account.
