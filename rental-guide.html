A LITTLE HELP GETTING HOME

Your next chapter.
One step at a time.

A new home starts with a few simple steps. Take a look around, get your questions answered, and apply when you find your fit.

  1. 01

    Find a place that feels right.

    Browse current homes for availability, pricing, and property details. See something you like? Call our team to discuss a showing.

    Browse available homes
  2. 02

    Know what to expect.

    Applications consider income, rental history, and screening. Before applying, review the requirements for your chosen home with our team, including current application details and any property-specific policies.

    Talk through your questions
  3. 03

    Take the next step.

    Open the home’s current listing and select “Apply Now.” Follow the application instructions, and contact us if you need a hand along the way.
